checked against at least two independent sources before it was written.

## A practical note

**Bukchon Hanok Village has a legal visitor curfew.** No visitors before 10am
or after 5pm, with fines up to ₩100,000. Her itinerary puts her there at
11:15, which is inside the window — but do not let the day slip.
